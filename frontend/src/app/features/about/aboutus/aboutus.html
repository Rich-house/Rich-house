Our Story

Menswear considered from every angle.

Rich House is an Egyptian menswear brand built around the confidence of a well-considered look. Our point of view brings together structured suits, occasionwear, and polished everyday pieces with a clear focus on proportion and fit.

From the first detail to the final silhouette, each choice is guided by craftsmanship, clarity, and the way clothing should feel when the occasion matters.
